Privacy Policy

At Bezawada Car Rentals, we take the confidentiality of your personal information, driving credentials, and booking data with highest dedication and transparency.

Your Privacy & Data Security are Guaranteed We never sell or disclose your personal data to commercial marketers. All verification records are strictly secured.
WhatsApp Helpline →

1. Information We Collect

To process reservations, verify legal driving authority, and comply with state transportation guidelines, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Identity & KYC Documentation: Clear copy of original, valid Driving License, Government-issued Identity Card (Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID), and recognized residential address proof (such as active Electricity/Power bill, postpaid mobile bill, or registered lease deed).
  • Foreign Guests: International Driving Permit (IDP), Passport with valid Visa endorsement copy.
  • Contact Details: Full Name, telephone/WhatsApp contact number, emergency family contact, and residential address.
  • Booking & Trip Details: Vehicle model requested, rental schedule (pickup and drop dates/times), dispatch hub or doorstep delivery address, and declared travel route.
  • Payment & Refund Data: UPI ID or Bank Account credentials provided strictly for processing the return of your security deposit within 2 working days.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used exclusively for lawful purposes related to vehicle rental execution and road safety:

  • Authenticating driving eligibility under the Motor Vehicles Act (age minimum 22 years, non-endorsed license).
  • Managing your reservation, coordinating vehicle handover at airport terminals or doorsteps, and generating invoices.
  • Facilitating prompt 24/7 on-road emergency assistance, breakdown support, and communication during your active road trip.
  • Reconciling highway FASTag toll debits and verifying traffic challans issued by state police authorities during the active tenure.
  • Refunding refundable security deposits back to your bank account or UPI within 2 business days following vehicle return inspection.

3. Vehicle Telematics & Safety Monitoring

For passenger security, fleet protection, and statutory compliance, rental vehicles are equipped with onboard telematics devices:

  • Odometer & Distance Tracking: Automated logging of distance driven to accurately verify 24-hour (400 km) and 12-hour (200 km) rental packages.
  • Highway Speed Monitoring: Onboard safety telemetry monitors highway cruising speed against the mandated 110 km/h speed threshold.
  • Theft Prevention: In the event of vehicle misappropriation, unauthorized route deviation into forbidden territories, or vehicle theft, location data may be shared with law enforcement authorities for asset recovery.
Privacy Assurance: Vehicle telematics data is reviewed strictly for speed compliance, vehicle maintenance, and emergency response. We never track or store personal conversations or passengers' private activities.

4. Information Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

Bezawada Car Rentals does not sell, rent, or lease your private information to third-party marketing companies. Information is shared strictly under the following limited conditions:

  • Legal & Police Authorities: When mandated by court summons, police investigation, statutory notices, or in response to hit-and-run incidents, traffic accidents, or unlawful activities during the rental period.
  • Insurance Providers & Surveyors: In the event of accidental damage exceeding policy thresholds, relevant KYC records and accident details are shared with authorized insurance claim assessors.
  • Operational Service Partners: Trusted authorized towing and breakdown service partners dispatched during emergency mechanical roadside breakdowns.

5. Data Security & Retention

We maintain strict administrative, digital, and physical safeguards to protect customer records against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or misuse:

  • Customer KYC documents and transaction records are retained for the period mandated under Indian tax, corporate, and statutory commercial accounting laws.
  • Following trip completion, challan reconciliation, and deposit refund, sensitive verification copies are securely archived with restricted operational access.
  • You may contact our support desk to verify or request correction of any outdated contact information held in our booking records.
